Fake It without Trying to Make It
Keep everyone on the same page
Have a daily, quick fire coordination meeting
Have a proforma standup
Make sure what you are building has value
Show it to ACTUAL USERS at least once a week and take their feedback as gospel; use that to drive the project plan
Have a monthly show and tell with management
Continuous delivery
Making sure valuable, reliable features are delivered to real users on a weekly basis
Having a CI tool running
Deliver quality software
Extensive end to end automated tests running on every build
Impressive code coverage metrics
Simple UX
Continually run the design past users who have never seen it, record what they do and review of how intuitive they find it
Have a UX review committee
Team Learning
Hold weekly team-led retrospectives that lead to real change and improvements in processes
Weekly meeting to complain about stuff
A culture of accountability
When things go wrong, everybody on the team looks in the mirror and asks what they personally could have done differently
Have an annual review where you are told what you did
Empowered Teams
Having empowered teams takes a lot of practices working together; but it is all dependent on creating a culture in which risk and failure are acceptable
Motivational notices on the walls
Embed the Client in the Team
Someone with knowledge and the ability to make decisions is physically present, interacting with all members of the team multiple times a day, often being involved in pairing and designing tests and UX
Have a point person who emails the clients updates and prepared questions maybe once a day
Collocated Teams
All together, facing each other or sharing screens, able to talk at any time without moving
On the same floor/in the same building, able to hold meetings if needed
Cross functional teams
All the skills needed are in the team, everyone sitting together, literally sharing tasks
An org chart has all these roles grouped together
High Performance Teams
Requires many of the other criteria here, but always having the deliberate intent to be a HPT, even if it means missing deadlines
Hiring "10x" people, putting them in teams and telling them to perform at a high level
Diversity
Ensure that the team has a wide variety of viewpoints onto the application domain so there are no blind spots
Have a checklist with token hires
Take risk up front
Explore technologies, build spikes, experiment, iterate
Fill out a risk assessment form
Agile organization (*)
Have every level of the organization look at and reflect on its performance from 360° and change appropriately
Buzzword compliance
Data driven organization (*)
Base all decisions on empirical data, and build systems capable of collecting that data: the data collected should be key to the running of the org (eg defect rates)
Collect some random statistics and post charts
Psychological Safety
Create a culture in which everyone feels that the truth will be respected and everyone is on a joint search for that
Make sure nobody ever feels challenged
Training Organization (*)
Multiple systems including mentoring, pairing, mobbing; communities of practice; sample projects to practise on; plenty of time dedicated to learning
Pay for staff to attend public training classes
(*) I have seen this tried but never achieved.
