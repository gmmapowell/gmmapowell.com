date=2020-11-08
written=2025-07-16


We are in quarantine.


We decided to come to the UK on US election day.  It turns out I was right about the result but wrong about the outcome.  For all the idiocy running rampant in the US, it seemed impossible to believe that a man who so callously lied about the risks of the pandemic and then watched people die could possibly win re-election when everything else he had done was so obviously self-serving.  But I had expected him to deny the result and organize his thousands (millions?) of thugs to march on Washington and force him to declare a state of emergency and hold onto power for another four years or so.  I did really expect to see riots in Washington on Wednesday morning.


Quarantine here is very different from what I've been used to.  We've been out and about every day during lockdown - there's plenty of space in the US and it's easy to avoid other people with or without a mask.  But, if I've understood the rules correctly, we are expected to stay inside all the time here during quarantine.  It's an odd thing, because we are in quarantine _at our flat_.  How, I hear you ask, did we get here?  In the end, we chose to come in a taxi as the least likely way of coming into contact with anybody.  We've ordered our groceries for delivery, but even then, they wouldn't bring them up and we had to go down and collect them.  But, we do our best, don't we?  At least lockdown is over here and we are looking forward to getting back to a normal life when we have completed quarantine.
