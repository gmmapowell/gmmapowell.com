Tech/on bugs mistakes and ai
* Difference between mistakes and bugs
* Unit testing catches mistakes
* You are focused on that
* Bugs come in through the cracks, like in a house. 
* Metaphor of complexity like a net
   * You test the ropes and the knots
   * But the problem is the gaps in between 
   * Bugs can climb through
   * It's easier to handle if the net is generally well made, but if it's a tangle... 
   * Refactoring is all about removing the tangles. 
* Example of a calculator 
   * This is deliberately trivial
   * We can do 2+2=4
   * And 3*5=15
   * But we make 3+4*5 possible without thinking about it... And it returns 35.
* Can AI help? 
   * Another analogy: pulling out on a freeway. Accidents happen because you have to look at 5 things at once. 
   * You need help watching complexity 
   * Current AI tools just do the opposite: they generate slop without watching for complexity. 
   * If I had a programming assistant, I would want it to be watching for and thinking about - and generating test cases for - those things.
