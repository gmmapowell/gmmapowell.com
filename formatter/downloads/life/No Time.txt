title=I Don't Have Time


It always annoys me when people say to me, "I don't have time".


Fair enough, yes, I am a generally ratty and easily annoyed person.  But the facts are: I don't like being lied to; I don't like lazy excuses; I don't like people hiding from reality; and I feel insulted by this kind of obvious deflection.


If that seems an intense response to a "perfectly normal statement", let me explain.


Every week has 168 hours in it.  This is a universal constant for everyone on planet Earth.  All of us have exactly the same amount of time.  The statement "I don't have time" is almost always code for "I don't want to waste my time doing that".


Now, if I explicitly said to you that I didn't want to "waste my valuable time spending it with you", I suspect you would be a little miffed.  But I insist that this is the only _reasonable_ interpretation of "I don't have time".  Now, it is true to say that I don't have enough time left in my life to build a full-size replica of the Parthenon in my back garden.  But, even so, that is only a partial truth: I also don't have a back garden; if I did it probably wouldn't be big enough; I don't have the money; and I don't have the skill.


But if I say "I don't have time to read your book and review it", what I'm really saying is that _of the 168 hours I have this week_, I am not prepared to use 8 of them helping you out.  And that's because this is too low a priority for me.  But the thing that _really_ bugs me is that people are not prepared to say that, and not prepared to get into a discussion of what their priorities are, and where I fit in.  They want to short-circuit the whole conversation by just saying "they don't have time".  It's dishonest.


I understand that just because I want something does not make it somebody else's top priority and nobody is obliged to help me out if they don't want to.  And I certainly understand that other people have things that either are more pressing or more enjoyable than whatever I want them to do.  But they can _say that_.  They do not need to lie and tell an obvious untruth because they think it will be more acceptable.


Because I don't have the time to deal with it 🙂
