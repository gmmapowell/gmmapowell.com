title=Guys is not Gender Neutral
saveAs=life/guys


The word "guys" or phrase "you guys" is everywhere now.


People seem to think that it is gender neutral and refers equally to men and women; as such, it is used as a replacement for "people", "folks" or just "you".  There's only one problem with this.


It isn't.


"Guys" is the plural of "guy" and, singular or plural, refers to a _male_, not a female.  Saying "you guys" is linguistically equivalent to "you men".  A few quick examples should make the point.


There is a musical "Guys and Dolls".  You may think this is about a group of young women and the dolls they play with.  No, it's about young men (guys) and young women (dolls).  If "guys" is gender neutral, so is "dolls".  Why don't people say "What do you want to do, dolls?" or "What are you dolls up to?"


The song "Dancing in the Street" contains the line "Every _guy_, grab a _girl_".  So, are we to interpret this as "every gender-neutral individual go and find an explicit female"?  Does that make sense?  No, it doesn't.


In the '80s (and possibly still today), there was an expression about female staff that they "just wanted to be one of the guys" either by staying late, drinking, or whatever.  It is a sentence that is clearly nonsensical if "guys" is gender neutral.


"Guys" is not gender neutral.  It refers to men.


Why does this matter?  It matters because, in spite of appearances, it is a subtle form of sexism and patriarchy.  It reflects the fact that women can be "absorbed" into being "lesser men" but it is strictly a one way street.  Again, replace "guys" with "ladies" or "dolls" and see how many men are prepared to go along with it.  And when you realize that, think about what that statement means.


And then please stop saying "guys" or "you guys".  In general, you don't need to say _anything_ there; just like you don't need to say someone's name.  If you are trying to attract everybody's attention, "everybody", "people", "peeps" and "folks" are all gender neutral.  If you are tempted to say "you guys", use the simpler "you".  It's just as effective.  If you really need the plural you, "all of you" will do the trick.


Just don't say "guys" unless you are referring explicitly to a group made up exclusively of males.
